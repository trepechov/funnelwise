# Recruiting pilot brands

## Who (plan 4.1, Q5)

Aim for 3 brands with different roles, at least one marketer who never uses a terminal:

| Slot | Profile | First skill tested |
|---|---|---|
| A | Creator or small media brand that sells sponsorships (YouTube + socials) | `media-kit` |
| B | Small business with a website that matters (has Search Console) | `seo-opportunities` |
| C | Social media / content manager for a brand (Instagram/TikTok/YouTube) | `content-performance` |

Requirements: Claude Pro or higher (Cowork), a Mac (Windows not supported in v0.1, see spike 0.2),
admin access to their own accounts, 90 minutes for the setup session plus 2 weeks of normal use.

## Invitation (adapt)

> I'm testing **Funnelwise**, a plugin for Claude that turns your own Google Analytics, Search
> Console, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and podcast numbers into ready-made reports: a sponsor media
> kit, a list of SEO fixes, or what's working on your social channels. It runs on your computer and
> only reads your data; it never posts anything and nothing is sent to me.
>
> I'm looking for 3 brands to try it for two weeks. It's free, I'll help you set it up in one
> 60–90 minute call, and you keep everything it makes. In return I'd like to watch the setup and
> hear what was useful and what wasn't. Interested?

## What pilots get

- Free setup help and the plugin.
- Their reports and data stay theirs (brand folder on their machine).
- A short summary of what other pilots found useful (anonymised).
